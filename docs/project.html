Project

A coordinated framework for renewing American cotton.

SmartCotton tests how regenerative practices, field-scale measurements, and digital technologies can move U.S. cotton toward a more resilient, climate-smart, and economically practical production system.

Research scope

SAS-CAP

USDA-NIFA coordinated agricultural project

Production focus

Cotton Belt

Climate-smart systems across major cotton regions

Core approach

Field + Data

Integrated trials, measurements, analytics, and extension

Research hypothesis

Regenerative management and precision tools can improve soil stewardship while supporting reliable cotton production.

Program Structure

Research organized around practical production decisions

The project connects field experiments, environmental measurements, analytics, and outreach so findings can move from research plots into grower-facing recommendations.

Soil Health

Track carbon, biology, structure, and long-term soil function.

Climate Metrics

Measure greenhouse gas emissions and sequestration potential.

Water Stewardship

Connect irrigation, rainfall, and crop response in cotton systems.

Pest Management

Study pest pressure, weed control, and resilient field practices.

Precision Agriculture

Use sensing and data layers to guide management decisions.

Grower Outcomes

Move research findings toward practical, extension-ready guidance.

Research Framework

From field evidence to climate-smart recommendations

1

Evaluate regenerative management in production-scale field systems.

2

Measure soil health, greenhouse gas, pest, nutrient, and water outcomes.

3

Translate results into decision support for practical cotton management.

Mission

Renew American cotton production by developing practical, science-based pathways for sustainable, resilient, and climate-smart systems.

Vision

A U.S. cotton sector that stewards land, improves soil health, lowers greenhouse gas emissions, and creates new market opportunities.

Objectives

Evaluate regenerative practices, improve input-use efficiency, enable precision management, and connect research outcomes to grower decision-making.

Research Framework

The project integrates field experiments, emissions measurement, pest and nutrient studies, water management, remote sensing, and AI/ML analytics.

Expected Outcomes

Evidence-based recommendations for carbon sequestration, reduced emissions, pest control, nutrient stewardship, water efficiency, and labor-aware management.

Impact

SmartCotton aims to improve sustainability and resilience across major cotton-producing states while supporting a reliable supply of climate-smart cotton.

Expected Impact

Evidence that can support resilient farms, healthier soils, and a stronger cotton supply.

SmartCotton is built to connect sustainability goals with field realities: carbon sequestration, lower emissions, input efficiency, pest and nutrient stewardship, water management, and labor-aware production practices.
