Ongoing Activities

Field research, extension, and technology demonstrations

Current activities connect plot- and field-scale measurements with grower engagement, technology evaluation, and decision-support resources.

Field Trials

Replicated trials across Cotton Belt ecoregions compare regenerative practices, tillage strategies, cover crops, and input programs.

Data Collection Campaigns

Teams collect soil, crop, emissions, pest, water, nutrient, and remote sensing measurements to support integrated analysis.

Workshops and Field Days

Extension activities translate research findings into practical guidance for growers, advisors, industry partners, and students.

Technology Demonstrations

Sensor platforms, analytics workflows, and AI/ML decision tools are evaluated for real-world cotton production use cases.
