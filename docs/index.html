Research Highlights

Selected areas of emphasis

Explore core research themes shaping the project, from soil carbon and greenhouse gas measurement to precision management and production resilience.

View all highlights

Soil systems

Soil Health and Carbon Sequestration

Field research evaluates how conservation tillage, cover crops, living mulches, and residue management can improve soil structure, microbial activity, and carbon storage in cotton systems.

Digital agriculture

AI/ML Precision Cotton Management

Researchers are developing data-driven approaches that combine remote sensing, field measurements, and machine learning to improve timing and placement of inputs.

Climate impact

Greenhouse Gas Reduction in Cotton

Multi-state trials measure emissions and management tradeoffs to identify practical pathways for reducing greenhouse gases while sustaining cotton productivity.

Project Goals

Improve cotton resilience while reducing the environmental footprint of production.

SmartCotton links field trials, extension, and data-driven management to support soil stewardship, greenhouse gas reduction, input-use efficiency, and climate-smart cotton supply.
