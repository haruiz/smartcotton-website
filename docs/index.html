SMARTCOTTON

Precision. Regeneration. Resilience.

SmartCotton is a USDA-NIFA Strengthening Agricultural Systems project bringing together researchers, Extension specialists, growers, and industry partners across the U.S. Cotton Belt to develop practical regenerative and precision-management strategies for more resilient cotton production.

USDA-NIFA SAS-CAP | Award No. 2024-68012-41750 | 2024-2029

Lead institution: Texas A&M AgriLife Research|PI/Lead: Dr. Muthukumar Bagavathiannan|Project Manager: Deepak Loura

Research in Action

Research in Action: Cover Crop Planting on Beds

Our SmartCotton team in College Station, Texas, is evaluating cover crop planting strategies for cotton grown on raised beds. Field observations, soil sampling, and crop measurements are helping researchers assess different management practices as part of a coordinated multi-state study.
